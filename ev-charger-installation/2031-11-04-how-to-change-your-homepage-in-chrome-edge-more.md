If you want to see a specific website when you open new browser windows or tabs, you can change your home page. The steps to change your home page vary by web browser, device, and platform, but the process is always very simple. This wikiHow guide will teach you how to change your web browser's home page on Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Firefox, and Safari on a computer, phone, or tablet.

Easily Change Your Homepage in Any Browser

[Change Your Homepage](https://change-your-homepage.northlist.shop/change-your-homepage/20260826.html)

Chrome: Go to ⋮ > Settings > Appearance, turn on "Show home button," and select a home page option.

Edge: Go to ⋯ > Settings > Start, home, and new tab page, turn on "Show the home button," and select a home page option.

Safari: Go to Safari > Settings > General and select a home page option.

Firefox: Go to ☰ > Settings > Home and choose a homepage option under "Homepage and new windows." Steps Method 1 Method 1 of 8: Google Chrome (Desktop) Download Article 1
