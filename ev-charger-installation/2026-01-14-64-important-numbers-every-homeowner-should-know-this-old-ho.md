For optimal viewing, size your TV based on its distance from the sofa. To get the minimum screen size in inches, divide the viewing distance by 3; for the maximum, divide by 1.5. Already have a TV? Use these guidelines to position your couch. Aim for a 15- to 20-degree viewing angle to the center of the screen.

Pro Tip: Steve Hayes, a media system designer featured in This Old House Magazine, warns: “Over and over, I see people put their television in a corner. It’s a huge mistake. You end up with bad view and bad sound.” Instead, think of a square layout with the viewer’s ideal seat directly in the center—the relationship between the TV and the primary seating is crucial.
