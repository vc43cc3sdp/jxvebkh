Pumpkins are a natural resource that can spawn in your Minecraft world. With just a set of shears, you can transform this block into a carved pumpkin—and we’re here to show you how. Read on to learn exactly how to carve pumpkins in Minecraft, as well as what you can do with them afterward. Things You Should Know
