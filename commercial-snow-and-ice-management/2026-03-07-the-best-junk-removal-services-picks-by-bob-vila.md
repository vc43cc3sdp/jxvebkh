While some of the top junk removal services are national companies with numerous locations, they won’t necessarily service every nook and cranny of the country. Pricing Structure

Most junk removal services base their rates on the overall size of the load or the number of items. Load size is typically determined by whether the job requires a ¼, ½, ¾, or full truckload (or multiple truckloads). Junk removal services may also charge by the item, typically for large pieces of furniture or appliances. Customers should determine which pricing structure works best for the items they need to be removed by getting quotes from multiple companies. Some companies will only provide a quote when they arrive to collect the unwanted junk, which may not appeal to some customers who would like to budget ahead of time. Disposal Methods

While price may be a crucial factor when choosing the best junk removal service, so too is selecting a service that will dispose of unwanted belongings responsibly. Customers should look for companies that will attempt to recycle or donate as much as possible as opposed to companies that simply dump all unwanted junk in the local landfill. Most junk removal services that use responsible disposal methods will proudly display that information on their website. Customers can also ask a customer service representative what happens to the junk, and the representative should clearly understand and communicate the process. Service Liability Coverage

Given that there are hazards when disposing of various types of junk, the junk removal service should have general liability insurance that covers any injuries their technicians may suffer while on the job. This coverage will also protect the homeowner from liability for any such injuries. Customer Support

A quality junk haul-away service will provide support by allowing customers to get in touch via phone, email, or, in some cases, live online chat. Before You Use One of the Best Junk Removal Services

Customers should consider all their junk removal options before deciding to use one of the best junk removal services. One alternative is dumpster rental, which is cheaper than a junk removal service but requires more work from the homeowner. The customer must dump all waste into a dumpster themselves and don’t have the benefit of a service that will recycle or donate items they don’t want. However, for situations like extended home improvement projects that are continually producing waste, renting a dumpster may make more sense than hiring a junk removal company.

When hiring a junk removal service, it’s also important to understand that these services won’t haul away junk of all kinds. Junk removal companies generally will not take hazardous materials, including old batteries, paint, and solvents. With this in mind, customers should learn what items the service will and will not take during the quote and booking process.

Cost of Using One of the Best Junk Removal Services

Junk removal is a relatively affordable service for the amount of work being done. Most junk removal services range between $50 and $361, with the national average costing around $233. Factors that impact the total cost of junk removal include the amount of junk and the type of junk being hauled off. Objects that are difficult to move, such as an old water heater, may cost more to dispose of than smaller items. Geographic location will also impact price, with higher costs in major metropolitan areas and lower costs in less populated areas. Junk removal in Chicago, for example, will likely cost more than junk removal in rural Pennsylvania.
