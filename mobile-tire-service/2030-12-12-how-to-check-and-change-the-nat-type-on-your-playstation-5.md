The PlayStation 5 is a powerful gaming console, but it can still run into internet connectivity issues from time to time. Your Network Address Translation, or NAT, can have a huge impact on your PS5's network speed. If your matchmaking is taking longer or your group chats are dropping, you may want to look at your PS5's NAT type. This wikiHow guide will help you change the NAT type on your PS5. Things You Should Know

To view your current NAT type, navigate to your PS5's Network Settings and select "View Connection Status".

An Open NAT type can connect to all other consoles, while a Strict NAT type can only connect to Open NAT consoles.

Change your NAT type by forwarding additional ports in your router's admin page. Steps Part 1 Part 1 of 2: Check Current NAT Type 1

Navigate to your PS5's Settings. Power on your PS5 if you have not already. Then, click on the gear icon in the top-right corner of the home menu to open your Settings.[1]XResearch source 2

[Type on](https://type-on.swapstreet.shop/type-on/202608265881.html)

Select "Network". Scroll through the different sections of your Settings menu until you find the Network option. Select it to continue.[2]XResearch source Advertisement 3

Select "View Connection Status". Under the Connection Status tab, you will see a few different options. Select View Connection Status to view your NAT type, amongst other relevant info.[3]XResearch source

You can also select Test Internet Connection to view your NAT type and troubleshoot any connectivity issues you may be experiencing. 4

View your NAT type. Your NAT determines the amount of connections you can make from your PS5. There are three NAT types:[4]XResearch source Open - Can connect to everything.

If you are experiencing network issues with an Open NAT type, there is no need to change your NAT type. Your issues likely lies elsewhere.

Moderate - Can connect to consoles with either an Open or Moderate NAT type.

Strict - can only connect to consoles with an Open NAT type.

If you have a Moderate or Strict NAT type and want to change it, you will need to use a process called port forwarding. Advertisement Part 2 Part 2 of 2: Change NAT Type (Port Forwarding) 1

Go to your router's admin page. To improve your PS5's NAT type, you will need to open additional ports on your router. There is nothing you can do through your PS5 to change its NAT type.

The exact port forwarding instructions will vary across different routers. This wikiHow guide goes into further depth on the process.

To access your router's admin page, enter its default gateway address into a web browser.[5]XResearch source 2

Add new ports. Add the following information to your router's port forwarding page:[6]XResearch source Protocol TCP: 1935, 3478-3480 Protocol UDP: 3074, 3478-3479

We also recommend adding your console's MAC and IP address on this page, guaranteeing that your router always recognizes your console's port access.

To find this information, return to the "View Connection Status" menu on your PS5.[7]XResearch source 4

Save your changes. Exit your router's admin portal, saving your changes as you do so. 5

Restart your router and your console. Restart both devices and then test your internet connection one last time. You should see an Open NAT type.
