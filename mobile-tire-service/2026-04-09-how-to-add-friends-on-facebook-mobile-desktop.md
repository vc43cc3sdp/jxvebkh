This wikiHow guide teaches you how to send and accept Facebook friend requests on both mobile and desktop platforms. You can easily add friends on Facebook on any computer, as well as on your Android, iPhone, or iPad. Once your friend request is accepted (or you accept another person's friend request), you'll be added to one another's Facebook friends lists. Quick Guide: Adding Friends on Facebook Search for the friend you want to add. Click or tap their profile. Click or tap Add Friend.

Wait for the person to accept your request.

Common Questions: Friend Requests on Facebook

How do I send a friend request on Facebook if there's no button?

If you don't see the Add Friend button, the person you want to be friends with may have disabled it in their settings. If so, you may want to ask them about it. However, if the other person says that they don't want to add you on Facebook (for whatever reason), be prepared to accept and respect their decision.

How do I update my Facebook friend request settings?

Go to your Facebook privacy settings and select Audience and Visibility > How People Find and Contact You. You can change the option next to Who can send you friend requests? to restrict who is allowed to send you friend requests to everyone, or just friends of friends.

How do I cancel a Facebook friend request?

If you want to cancel a friend request you sent, just go to that person's Facebook profile and click the Cancel Request button (which is where the Add Friend button used to be). To delete a request you got, click on the friend request notification and click Delete next to the request. Tips
