Proper installation prep is critical for long-lasting performance. On the This Old House TV Cambridge project, contractor Tommy Silva and carpenter Josh demonstrated exactly what thorough window prep looks like: after removing damaged framing, they went “all the way down to the rim joist in some areas,” then reframed the openings completely and applied waterproofing on the outside with a bead of backer rod on the bottom—ready to set the new window on a solid, weather-tight foundation.

Beyond the beauty of their new windows and doors, the homeowners were happy to talk about some of their favorite aspects of working with Renewal. For one, they appreciated the care the crew took to protect their furniture and floors, covering everything to protect it from damage and dust. Also, when it came to their custom window and specialty patio door, they were relieved to find a company that could not only supply the materials but also handle the installation. They also mentioned the fact that they could choose their hardware and colors was a big plus for their home.

The homeowners were even happier to learn that their new windows and doors are maintenance-free. Made from Andersen’s Fibrex material, they don’t require any staining or sealing for their lifetime.
