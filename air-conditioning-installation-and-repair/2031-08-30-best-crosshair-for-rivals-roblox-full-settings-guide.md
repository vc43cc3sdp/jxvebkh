Rivals is one of the most popular head-to-head games on Roblox, and having an accurate aim can make you so much better at the game. Even though your crosshair depends on your personal preference and what works for you while playing, there are some crosshair settings that can help improve your game. We’ve partnered with a Roblox streamer to help us walk you through the best crosshair shapes to try out, how to change them, and other ways to improve your aim. Top Crosshair Design Ideas in Rivals

Roblox Streamer D'Andre Bridges says the best crosshair in Rivals depends on what works well for you and what sensitivity you’re used to. In general, smaller designs (like dots, crosses, or Xs) work best to improve your aim. Choose a bright color to help it stand out so it’s easier to line up your shots. How to Change Crosshair Settings in Rivals Download Article

Go into the Crosshair tab in the game’s settings menu. When you’re in the game, click the gear icon in the top right corner to open the settings. Then, tap on the Crosshair tab to access all of the settings. As you adjust the settings, a preview appears on screen so you know what your crosshair will look like while you’re in a match. Section 3 of 3: How to Improve Your Aim in Rivals Download Article 1

Use a bright-colored crosshair. Crosshairs that have a bright or neon color stand out against the background more, so it’s easier to see where you’re aiming. If you’re having trouble lining up your shots or finding your crosshair on the screen, switch it to something like bright red, neon green, hot pink, or light blue. 2

Adjust your look sensitivity until you’re accurate. Go into the game settings under the General tab and scroll down until you reach the Sensitivity setting. Start by lowering your sensitivity first so you can practice landing on your target while you’re aiming. As you get more comfortable on a lower sensitivity, gradually increase it so you’re able to turn and maneuver faster.[9]XResearch source

If you’re playing on mobile, you may also turn up the Aim Assist Strength to help you line up your shots better.

According to Bridges, sensitivity is the most crucial factor. “I like a fast sensitivity,” he says. “I feel like if I move faster and hit precise shots, I'll be able to hit the shots better.”[10]XExpert SourceD'Andre Bridges Roblox StreamerExpert Interview 3

Change your mouse speed in your computer settings. When you’re playing on a computer, adjusting your pointer speed can help you move your mouse more accurately. On a Windows computer, go into Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Mouse and use the Mouse Pointer Speed slider to change the settings. On a Mac, go to System Settings → Mouse and change the Tracking Speed slider.[11]XResearch source

Some gaming mouses also come with their own software that you can adjust the dots per inch (DPI) to raise or lower sensitivity. Keep the setting between 400-800 DPI for the best results.

After you change your mouse settings, try to stick with them for at least 2 weeks so you can get used to it. If you’re still having trouble after that, make more adjustments. 4

Practice with an aim trainer. Aim trainers help you practice so you can line up your shots faster and more accurately. Use something like 3D Aim Trainer to run drills online directly from your browser or use the REFLEX Aim Trainer Roblox experience to practice in game.
