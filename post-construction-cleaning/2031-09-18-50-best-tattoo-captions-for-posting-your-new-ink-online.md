You just got some fresh ink, and you’re ready to show it off to the world on social media! But what should you say? How do you caption a piece of art so special that you’ve gotten it permanently etched onto your skin? We’re here with all the best caption ideas, from the deep to the funny to the short ‘n’ sweet, and everything in between. Coming Up with Good Tattoo Captions

Go with a simple caption if you want to let the tattoo be the star of the show: “Really love my new ink.”

Choose a funny caption to let others know you want to be playful: “Way better than the stick-figure sketch I gave them.”
